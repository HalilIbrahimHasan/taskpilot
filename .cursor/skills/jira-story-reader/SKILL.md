# Jira Story Reader

1. Extract the user outcome, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and exclusions.
2. Resolve contradictions against `AGENTS.md` and product requirements.
3. Identify database, API, UI, test, and documentation impacts.
4. Require explicit approval before implementing STORY-A or STORY-B.
5. Produce a traceable checklist and note unanswered product decisions.

Do not claim Jira access unless the story was actually retrieved through an available integration.
